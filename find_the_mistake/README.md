**Pairs · 5 minutes · no code needed**

**Question:** "My mom is 70 and needs a ride to her doctor next Tuesday, and we're low on groceries this week. What can we do?"

**Guide:** [`../data/city_guide.md`](../data/city_guide.md) (Senior Ride Share + Pantry Pass sections)

**Agent's answer:**

> Two programs can help. **Senior Ride Share** gives free rides to medical appointments for anyone 65 and up. Since her appointment is next Tuesday, call 555-0198 at least 48 hours ahead, so by Sunday at the latest.
>
> For groceries, **Pantry Pass** at the Riverside Community Center, 120 Elm St, is open Tuesday and Sunday, 9am to 1pm. No ID is needed, and you can go once per household per week. You could stop by Tuesday on the way back from the doctor.
