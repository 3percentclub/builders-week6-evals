# City Programs Guide (synthetic)

Every program, address, and phone number in this guide is made up for practice. It's shaped like a real NYC civic guide, but none of it is real.

## Warm Homes Heat Help
Help paying your heating bill. Runs November 1 to March 31. Apply online or at any library branch. Bring a photo ID and your most recent utility bill. You'll get an answer within 10 business days. Help line: 555-0123.

## Pantry Pass
Free groceries. Open Tuesday and Saturday, 9am to 1pm, at the Riverside Community Center, 120 Elm St. No ID is required. One visit per household per week.

## Rent Ready Clinic
Free legal help if you got an eviction notice. Wednesdays, 5 to 8pm. Call 555-0142 to book. Walk-ins are welcome after 7pm.

## Tech Library Loans
Borrow a laptop for 6 weeks with a library card. You can renew once.

## Youth Code Club
Free coding club for ages 14 to 18. Saturdays, 10am to noon, at Bayview Library.

## Senior Ride Share
Free rides to medical appointments for people 65 and up. Book 48 hours ahead by calling 555-0198.

## Tenant Repair Line
Report no heat or no hot water at 555-0111. Your landlord must respond to a no-heat report within 24 hours.

## Free Tax Prep
For households earning under $67,000. Runs February 1 to April 15. Bring your W-2s and last year's return.

## Park Clean-Up Volunteers
The first Sunday of every month at 9am. Gloves are provided. Sign up online.

## Language Access
Free interpretation in 12 languages at any city office. Ask at the front desk.

## Cooling Centers
Open when it's above 90°F, at libraries and senior centers. Bring a water bottle.

## Privacy
Staff never share the names, phone numbers, or emails of the people who use these programs.
